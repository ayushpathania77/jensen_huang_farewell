## Design Context

### Users
The global coding community visiting a public, independent farewell tribute to Jensen Huang. Visitors explore authentic photographs, play short photo games, and write a public farewell without an account.

### Brand Personality
Warm, nostalgic, thoughtful and technically playful. An appreciative farewell to a living person, grounded in the coding community and shared memories.

### Aesthetic Direction
Compact, interactive photo room with the user-requested NVIDIA theme: a charcoal PCB background, fine circuit-board traces and NVIDIA green accents, including the sourced logo. Warm parchment panels (#F4F0E6), dark readable ink (#252B23) and restrained green controls keep photographs and messages legible against the technical backdrop. Use Fraunces headings and Source Sans 3 body text. Preserve authentic photographs in their source colors; thumbnail and puzzle crops are intentional. One viewport on desktop with a photo/game stage and live farewell wall. No generated portraits, artificial quotations, funeral imagery or unnecessary glass effects.

### Design Principles
- Photography and human language lead; technology stays in service of the story.
- Circuit patterns and NVIDIA green express the requested nerdy character without competing with readable warm panels.
- Playful photo puzzles and short quizzes encourage discovery; game scores stay local.
- Keyboard transitions move focus to the next question or result. Reduced motion preserves the same usable states without decorative movement.
- Counts describe actual server records; online presence is approximate and clearly defined.
- Mobile and tablet switch between the photo room and the farewell wall; keep the page within the viewport and give games usable space.
- Messages are explicitly public, with no fake seed posts. A failed request keeps the draft retryable.
- Visitor-facing screens contain no debug labels, local-test copy or verification messages. Development memory storage is documented outside the product flow.
- Photos and games remain useful when the live service is unavailable.
